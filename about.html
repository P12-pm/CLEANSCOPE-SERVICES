Who We Are

An Australian Cleaning Company Built on Experience

CleanScope Services is an Australian cleaning company built on more than 20 years of combined industry experience. Our leadership team brings extensive hands-on experience across aged care, hospitality, commercial and office environments, industrial facilities and strata properties.

We understand that every environment is different and requires a cleaning approach that meets its specific needs. From maintaining high standards of hygiene and presentation in aged care and hospitality settings to delivering reliable cleaning solutions for commercial, industrial and strata properties, we focus on quality, consistency and attention to detail.
