Work Health & Safety

CLEANSCOPE Services is committed to a strong safety culture. We provide clear, practical training and guidance both onsite and digitally, ensuring our team understands safe work practices and expectations. Our approach promotes consistent, compliant and safe operations, protecting our employees, clients, visitors and the communities we serve.

Quality Management

CLEANSCOPE Services Quality Management Framework ensures consistent standards and reliable service delivery. Our supervisors conduct regular site inspections and provide detailed reports, supported by audits, corrective action plans and follow-up reviews where required. We also undertake ongoing compliance checks and internal benchmarking to maintain high standards and drive continuous improvement across all client facilities.

Infection Prevention & Control

Infection prevention and control is central to the CLEANSCOPE Services approach. Our experienced team follows recognised best practices, with staff receiving appropriate internal and external training.
