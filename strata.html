About Our Approach

Creating Cleaner, Safer & More Welcoming Properties

A well-maintained strata property creates a cleaner, safer and more welcoming environment for residents, visitors and property managers. At CleanScope Services, we provide reliable strata cleaning solutions tailored to the unique needs of each property.

From apartment buildings and residential complexes to mixed-use developments, we understand that common areas need to look their best every day. Our experienced teams deliver consistent cleaning across high-traffic areas while working around residents and building operations.

We work with strata managers, owners' corporations and property managers to develop a cleaning schedule that suits the building, occupancy and budget.
